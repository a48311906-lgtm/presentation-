Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What are the check-in and check-out timings?

Check-in is from 11:00 AM and check-out is by 10:00 PM at both properties. Early check-in or late check-out can be arranged on request, subject to availability.

What do EP, CP, MAP and AP mean on the tariff?

EP is room only, CP includes breakfast, MAP adds one more meal (lunch or dinner), and AP includes breakfast, lunch and dinner. All plans are quoted per room, per night.

Is parking available at both hotels?

Yes, both Kreston Heights Indore (Sch 78) and Kreston Heights Bhopal offer free on-site parking for guests.

Do you offer airport or railway station pickup?

Pickup and drop can be arranged for an additional charge at both locations. Please share your travel details in advance on WhatsApp or by phone so we can plan accordingly.

What is your cancellation policy?

Bookings can be cancelled free of charge up to 24 hours before check-in. Cancellations made closer to the date, or no-shows, may attract a charge equal to one night's tariff.

Are the tariffs inclusive of taxes?

Displayed tariffs are exclusive of applicable government taxes, which will be added at the time of billing.

Do you allow extra beds or child stays?

An extra bed can be added to most room categories for an additional charge. Children below 5 years can stay free of charge using existing bedding.
